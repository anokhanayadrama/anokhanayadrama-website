08 / FAQ

Common questions.

Is AnokhaNayaDrama completely vegetarian?+

Yes. AnokhaNayaDrama is focused on pure vegetarian food guidance and recipes for babies, adults and families.

What kind of baby-food guidance is available?+

Practical questions about age-appropriate foods, meal ideas, ingredients, spices, portions, textures and everyday feeding routines. Medical, allergy or growth concerns should be discussed with a pediatrician or qualified dietitian.

Can I get recipes for the whole family?+

Yes. AnokhaNayaDrama covers Indian home-style adult and family recipes, including quick meals and ways to adapt a family dish for a younger eater.

Do you provide medical nutrition advice?+

No. The service is for general food guidance and recipe planning, not diagnosis or treatment. For medical conditions, allergies, feeding difficulties or growth concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
