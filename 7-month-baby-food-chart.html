7 Month Baby Food Chart: Indian Vegetarian Meal Ideas

Wondering what to feed your 7-month-old baby? Here are simple homemade vegetarian meal ideas to help you plan complementary feeding alongside breast milk or infant formula.

Sample 1-Day Baby Food Chart

MealFood idea
MorningBreast milk or infant formula
BreakfastSoft, well-cooked ragi porridge
LunchSoft moong dal and rice khichdi
EveningBreast milk or infant formula
DinnerOptional small serving of mashed, well-cooked vegetables, depending on baby's readiness and usual feeding routine

This is an example, not a fixed feeding schedule. At 6–8 months, many babies gradually work toward 2–3 complementary meals daily while continuing milk feeds. Follow your baby's hunger and fullness cues.

3 Easy Indian Vegetarian Food Ideas

1. Moong Dal Khichdi

Cook washed rice and yellow moong dal with plenty of water until very soft. Mash thoroughly and adjust the texture to your baby's developmental readiness.

2. Ragi Porridge

Cook plain ragi flour thoroughly with water, stirring until smooth. Cool before serving. Avoid adding sugar or honey.

3. Mashed Vegetables

Steam carrot, pumpkin or sweet potato until very soft. Mash to a suitable texture and offer in small amounts.

Important Feeding Safety Tips

  • Continue breast milk or infant formula alongside complementary foods.
  • Offer iron-rich foods regularly, such as well-cooked lentils and iron-fortified infant cereals.
  • Avoid honey before 12 months, added salt and sugar, and choking hazards such as whole nuts.
  • Introduce common allergens in safe, age-appropriate forms, one at a time, and watch for reactions. Seek medical advice first for babies with severe eczema or known food allergy.
  • Always supervise your baby while eating and use a safe upright seated position.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a 7-month-old baby eat khichdi?

Yes, when cooked until very soft and served in an age-appropriate texture. Avoid hard pieces and choking hazards.

Does my baby still need milk?

Yes. Breast milk or infant formula remains important throughout the first year.
